Our Homestyle Culinary Story

Connecting food lovers with the comfort of authentic, nutritious, home-cooked food since 2018.

Founded With Love

From A Mother's Kitchen To Ahmedabad's Most Trusted Tiffin Service

Shrihari Tiffin was born out of a simple observation: students, young professionals, and busy families in Ahmedabad were forced to compromise on their health by eating oily restaurant food every day.

In 2018, we started preparing 20 homestyle tiffins daily using traditional Gujarati recipes, 100% pure ghee, cold-pressed groundnut oil, and fresh spices. Today, our centralized, FSSAI-certified kitchen serves over 1,500 fresh thalis every single day!

“Food is not just sustenance — it is emotion. Every tiffin we pack carries the warmth and care of a mother's home cooking.”

— Founder & Master Chef, Shrihari Kitchens

Uncompromising Hygiene

5-Point Kitchen Purity Promise

We maintain rigorous hygiene protocols that match international kitchen standards.

1

Daily Kitchen Sanitization

Deep steam cleaning and UV sterilization of all prep counters, utensils, and stainless steel tiffin containers before every meal shift.

2

100% RO Purified Water

All cooking, washing, dal prep, and dough kneading is conducted exclusively using industrial RO-purified water.

3

Chef Safety Gear

Our kitchen staff wears mandatory hairnets, face shields, gloves, and aprons at all times during cooking and packing.

4

Farm-Fresh Sourcing

Vegetables are sourced daily at 5:00 AM from local organic markets and washed in ozone fruit-and-veg purifiers.

5

Thermal Temperature Control

Tiffins are heat-sealed immediately after cooking to ensure food arrives piping hot (>65°C) without losing aroma or vitamins.
