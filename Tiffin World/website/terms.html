1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using TiffinWorld.com (the "Platform"), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. Please review them carefully before placing meal inquiries or subscribing to vendor plans.

2. Marketplace Service Model

TiffinWorld operates as a curated online marketplace connecting independent home chefs ("Kitchen Partners") with customers seeking daily home-cooked meal subscriptions. TiffinWorld facilitates inquiry matching and platform quality standards.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

Meal subscriptions can be paused or resumed up to 8 hours before scheduled meal delivery windows. All financial transactions conducted through the platform are processed using encrypted gateway standards.

4. Food Quality & Hygiene

All Kitchen Partners on TiffinWorld are required to maintain active FSSAI registration and adhere to strict sanitation and packaging guidelines.

5. Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellations requested prior to daily cut-off times are credited to your TiffinWorld wallet balance for future meal plan orders.
