PSB Multiplier (Phosphate Solubilizing Biofertilizer)

PSB Multiplier is a research-backed microbial biofertilizer containing high-potency Pseudomonas striata and Bacillus megaterium strains. It solubilizes insoluble soil phosphorus, converting tied-up minerals into plant-available orthophosphate for rapid root growth, early flowering, and yield increases up to 30%.

Detailed Product Information

Phosphorus is a critical plant macro-nutrient responsible for early root initiation, stalk strength, and uniform seed setting. However, over 75% of applied chemical DAP/SSP fertilizer gets bound with soil aluminum, iron, and calcium salts.

  • Drip Fertigation: Mix recommended dose in 200L water tank; apply through drip during early vegetative stage.

Keep pouch/bottle sealed in a cool, dry place below 30°C. Safe for earthworms, livestock, and beneficial insects.

Q: Can PSB Multiplier replace chemical DAP completely?
A: It reduces chemical DAP requirement by 30-40% in season 1, while unlocking years of accumulated phosphorus residue in the soil.

Vise Organic PSB Multiplier is engineered in our DSIR-approved biotechnology laboratories. It forms a symbiotic mycorrhizal-bacterial bridge around roots, helping crops absorb locked nutrients even under moisture stress.
