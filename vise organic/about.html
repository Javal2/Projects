OUR FOUNDING JOURNEY

Combining Organic Warmth With Scientific Rigor

Founded in Gujarat, India, Vise Organic emerged from a fundamental goal: to replace toxic synthetic chemicals with high-efficiency microbial inoculants and botanical biopesticides that nourish the soil while multiplying crop profitability.

Over the past decade, our dedicated team of agricultural microbiologists, agronomists, and bio-process engineers have developed DSIR-recognized microbial formulations that increase soil organic carbon, boost nutrient uptake efficiency, and deliver zero-residue crop protection.
