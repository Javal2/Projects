### About me

I'm **Javal**, a UI/UX designer working at the intersection of **interface design, websites and practical automation**. I care about clean layouts, thoughtful interactions, strong visual hierarchy and experiences that feel effortless to use.

- 🎨 **Design:** UI/UX, product interfaces, responsive web design, Figma and Framer
- 🛠️ **Build:** websites, landing pages and frontend experiences
- ⚡ **Automate:** n8n and marketing workflows
- 📊 **Explore:** SQL and Power BI
- 🌍 **Based in:** Gujarat, India
- 🤝 **Open to:** design projects, collaborations and digital experiences
