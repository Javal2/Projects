Understanding USP Monograph Revisions for Caffeine Anhydrous (CAS 58-08-2)

ER
Dr. Elena Rostova
Senior Regulatory Compliance Officer • Bulkpharmachem QA
Fig 1.1: ISO 17025 High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) assay run for Caffeine Anhydrous.
Executive Summary & Key Takeaways
  • Stationary Phase Shift: C18 reverse-phase HPLC columns with acetonitrile/water gradient are now mandatory.
  • Heavy Metal Limits: Lead (Pb), Arsenic (As), Cadmium (Cd), and Mercury (Hg) ICP-MS caps enforced at < 1.0 ppm.
  • Batch Assurance: 100% of dispatched lots from Bulkpharmachem hubs undergo full re-assay prior to container seal.

The United States Pharmacopeia (USP) has published updated testing criteria regarding chromatographic purity limits, organic volatile impurities (OVIs), and heavy metal ICP-MS quantification for bulk Caffeine Anhydrous (CAS 58-08-2).

1. Key Analytical Method Changes

The revised monograph shifts standard liquid chromatography (HPLC) stationary phase requirements to C18 reverse-phase columns with a tighter acetonitrile/water gradient. This ensures zero co-elution of residual xanthine or methylxanthine precursors during batch analysis.

Bulkpharmachem Certificate of Analysis Guarantee

All Caffeine Anhydrous batches dispatched from our Rotterdam and New Jersey distribution facilities undergo 100% verification under these revised USP/EP criteria prior to container seal.

2. Heavy Metal ICP-MS Thresholds

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) limits for Lead (Pb), Arsenic (As), Cadmium (Cd), and Mercury (Hg) remain strictly capped under 1.0 ppm, exceeding generic industrial benchmarks.

3. Loss on Drying (LOD) Requirements

Thermal desiccation testing at 105°C for 4 hours must yield a moisture loss of no greater than 0.5% w/w, safeguarding powder flowability during high-speed tablet compression.
