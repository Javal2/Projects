Container Shipping & Freight Incoterms

We support FOB (Free on Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance, & Freight), CFR, DDP, and EXW shipment terms according to ICC Incoterms 2020 definitions.

Quality Assurance Claims & Return Protocols

If a delivered batch fails lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (CoA) or monograph specifications upon destination port arrival, quality claims must be submitted within 30 business days accompanied by an independent ISO 17025 accredited laboratory test report.
