FlemmingCef™ 500 Tablets

Cefuroxime Axetil USP 500 mg Film-Coated Tablets

FlemmingCef™ 500 is a high-precision, broad-spectrum second-generation cephalosporin antibiotic. Manufactured in a dedicated contamination-free cephalosporin block equipped with positive pressure HEPA air handling systems.

Active Ingredient (API) Cefuroxime Axetil USP eq. to Cefuroxime 500 mg
Dosage Form Film-Coated Tablet
Therapeutic Category Anti-Infective / Cephalosporin Antibiotic
Packaging Standard 10 x 1 x 10 Alu-Alu Blister Pack / Export Carton
Shelf Life 36 Months (3 Years)
Dossier Availability Ready CTD / eCTD Format
Indications & Clinical Application

FlemmingCef™ 500 is indicated for the treatment of acute bacterial sinusitis, acute otitis media, pharyngitis, tonsillitis, acute bacterial exacerbations of chronic bronchitis, and uncomplicated skin structure infections.

Bioequivalence & Dissolution Standards

Formulated to match the innovator dissolution profile in multi-pH media (pH 1.2, pH 4.5, pH 6.8). Manufactured using USP-grade active pharmaceutical ingredients sourced from certified API partners.

Export Licensing & CTD Dossiers

Complete Module 1 to 5 CTD dossiers available with zone IVb stability data (30°C / 75% RH) ready for submission to MOH regulatory authorities in Latin America, Africa, and CIS countries.
